# K1 Advanced Storage — Factorio 2.1

More room for your factory! Add spacious **2×2 and 6×6 chests**, including all five logistic variants, with artwork originally created for **[Krastorio Legacy](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Krastorio)**.

This is an **unofficial Factorio 2.1 continuation** of [K1 Advanced storage continued](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/K1_Advanced_storage_continued), maintained by **Spawnsen**.

## Features

- **12 chest variants** across two sizes.
- Configurable inventory sizes through startup settings.
- Circuit connections on every chest.
- Configurable trash inventories for requester and buffer chests.
- English, German and Russian translations.

| Chest | Footprint | Default inventory* | Configurable range |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Medium | 2×2 tiles | 200 slots | 100–200 slots |
| Large | 6×6 tiles | 2,000 slots | 800–2,000 slots |

*At normal quality.*

Both sizes are available as **regular, storage, buffer, requester, active provider and passive provider** chests.

## Research and Space Age

Unlock larger chests, simple logistic chests and advanced logistic chests through three research stages.

With **Space Age** enabled, advanced chest research uses space science packs. The chests cannot be placed on space platforms.

## Compatibility and upgrading

- Requires **Factorio 2.1.7 or newer within the 2.1 series**.
- No additional third-party mods are required.
- Tested with **Factorio 2.1.23**, with and without Space Age, Quality, Recycler and Elevated Rails.

This fork uses the unique internal name `Spawnsen_K1_Advanced_Storage`. When switching from **K1 Advanced storage continued**, including versions 2.1.0 and 2.1.1 of this fork, **disable the old mod and enable this one before loading your save**. Chest, item, recipe, technology and startup-setting names are preserved. Try the switch on a copy of your save first.

The original storage mods and this continuation cannot be enabled together because they define the same chest prototypes.
